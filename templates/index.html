How the Matching Pipeline Works

Deterministic, interpretable NLP with no hidden black boxes

01

In-Memory Extraction

Parses PDF, DOCX, and TXT streams entirely in RAM without storing files on disk, ensuring serverless compatibility and privacy.

02

Taxonomy & Phrase Matching

Multi-word boundary matching across 8 tech categories (Languages, Frameworks, Cloud, Databases) with synonym normalization.

03

TF-IDF Vectorization

Transforms normalized text and job specifications into term-frequency inverse-document-frequency vectors with n-gram support.

04

Explainable Scoring

Calculates Cosine Similarity and Skill Match Rate to output an objective, balanced relevance score with complete breakdown.

💡

Scientific & Algorithmic Transparency

Resume Analyzer's relevance score is calculated as a composite index: 50% TF-IDF Cosine Similarity (evaluating vocabulary and contextual keyword overlap) and 50% Technical Skill Match Rate (evaluating specific required skills).

We deliberately do not market our score as an "Official ATS Pass Score" or "Hiring Probability", because authentic recruitment involves human judgment, behavioral interviews, and work portfolios.
